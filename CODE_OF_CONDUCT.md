# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone participating in LogSlice, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, is expected to help keep the community welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, **Yagnang Vyas**, privately through GitHub ([https://github.com/yagnang-vyas](https://github.com/yagnang-vyas)). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take any action considered appropriate, including:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from the project community.
